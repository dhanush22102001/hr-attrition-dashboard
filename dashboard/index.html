Key insights

  • Attrition is highest in frontline teams: Customer Support (29.2%) and Sales (29.0%), vs Engineering (9.1%).
  • The first two years are the danger zone: 30.6% attrition for 0–2 year employees — an onboarding and early-career progression signal.
  • Career growth is the #1 stated exit reason (19 of 48), ahead of compensation — clear progression paths may retain more people than pay rises alone.
  • Low job-satisfaction and work-life-balance scores cluster among leavers in the dataset (see analysis script).
